Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Wipezy ("we", "our", or "us") is a media management application that helps users browse, organize, review, and remove unwanted media files stored on their devices.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Wipezy accesses, how information may be processed, and the choices available to you when using the application.

1. Information We Access

Media Files

Wipezy may access images, videos, GIFs, audio files, documents, and other supported media stored on your device, depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant. This access allows the app to display media, show file sizes, organize supported files, identify duplicate images, and help you remove unwanted files.

Your media files are processed locally on your device where the relevant features support on-device processing. Wipezy does not intentionally upload your personal media files to our servers as part of its normal media-browsing and cleaning functionality. If a future feature requires transferring data, we will update this policy as appropriate.

Device and Diagnostic Information

If diagnostic or crash-reporting services are enabled, technical information may be collected when the app encounters an error. This information may include crash logs, app version, device model, operating system version, and diagnostic details needed to identify and fix problems.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed or collected may be used to:

  • Display and organize supported media files.
  • Identify duplicate images and help users review files.
  • Allow users to select and delete unwanted media.
  • Diagnose crashes, investigate technical issues, and improve stability.
  • Provide and maintain premium features and subscriptions.
  • Respond to user questions and support requests.

3. On-Device Processing and Storage

Wipezy is designed to perform media management operations on your device wherever possible. Information used for media browsing or classification may be processed locally, depending on the feature.

App settings and other operational information may be stored locally on your device. We do not claim that all information remains on your device: information handled by enabled third-party services may be processed according to their respective privacy policies.

4. File Deletion

Wipezy may allow you to delete selected media files. Deletion may be subject to Android permissions, system confirmation dialogs, and device-specific restrictions.

Please review your selection carefully before confirming deletion. Deleted files may not be recoverable through Wipezy. We are not responsible for files you choose to delete.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Wipezy may offer paid subscriptions or premium digital features. Payments made through Google Play are handled by Google Play and are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.

We do not directly collect or store your full payment card details when payments are processed by Google Play. We may receive limited purchase or subscription information needed to provide premium features, verify purchase status, or manage subscription access, depending on the billing implementation.

6. Third-Party Services

Wipezy may use third-party services to support app functionality, subscriptions, crash reporting, or diagnostics. These services may process certain technical or transaction-related information under their own privacy policies.

Depending on the services enabled in the app, these may include:

  • Google Play services and Google Play Billing: Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics, if enabled: Firebase Privacy and Security

7. Advertising and Google AdMob

Wipezy uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements within the application. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information to provide, deliver, measure, and improve advertisements.

Depending on your device settings, consent choices, and the advertising configuration used in Wipezy, this information may include:

  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers.
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system, language, and app version.
  • Information about ad impressions, interactions, and advertising performance.
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Diagnostic, fraud-prevention, and security-related information.

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, limit repeated ads, prevent invalid advertising activity, and support advertising personalization where permitted by applicable law and your consent choices.

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be shown personalized or non-personalized advertisements. You can manage available advertising privacy settings through your Android device settings and any consent options presented within the app.

Google may process information as an independent service provider under its own privacy policies and applicable terms. For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google AdMob policies and guidance

Only list services that are actually integrated into the published version of Wipezy. Other third-party services will be disclosed here as applicable.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate app features, provide subscriptions, diagnose crashes, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not intentionally share your personal media files with third parties as part of normal local media management.

9. Data Retention

Media files remain on your device unless you choose to delete them or another device-level process removes them.

Diagnostic information collected by third-party services may be retained according to the applicable service's retention settings and policies. Locally stored app information can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application, subject to Android and third-party service behavior.

10. Permissions and Your Choices

You can manage Wipezy's permissions through your Android device settings. If you deny or revoke media access, some app features may not work as intended.

You can stop using Wipezy at any time by uninstalling the app. You may also manage subscriptions through Google Play.

11. Children's Privacy

Wipezy is not specifically directed at children under the age required by applicable law to provide consent independently. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

12. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by Wipezy. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
